Thực hiện milestone theo format chuẩn FAANG.

## FORMAT MỖI MILESTONE

### 💡 The Why
- Business Purpose: giải quyết business need gì?
- Technical Purpose: về kỹ thuật đạt được gì?
- Risks (Likelihood × Impact × Mitigation)
- Trade-offs (Option A vs B → Decision → Lý do)
- Alternatives Considered (Pros / Cons / Why not)

### 🛠 The How
- Step-by-step chi tiết
- Pseudocode
- Sample Code (có comment)
- Best Practices (✅)
- Common Pitfalls (❌)

### 📊 Multi-Role Review
- BA: Business requirement? Stakeholder? KPI?
- DA: Data quality? Analytics readiness? Metric clear?
- DE: Reliability? Scale x10/x100/x1000? Fault tolerance?
- AE: Model correctness? Semantic layer? SSOT?
- Dev: SOLID/DRY? Security? Tests? Tech debt? Performance?

### 🏁 Knowledge Check
3-5 câu hỏi hoặc bài tập kiểm tra hiểu biết.

### 📝 Lessons Learned
What Worked / What Failed / Trade-offs / Tech Debt / Future Improvements
